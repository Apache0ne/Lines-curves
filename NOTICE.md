The model architecture is derived from the public TEED implementation by Xavier Soria and collaborators:

- Repository: https://github.com/xavysp/TEED
- Paper: *Tiny and Efficient Model for the Edge Detection Generalization*, ICCV Workshops 2023.

CurveML is maintained separately at https://gitlab.com/4ndr3aR/CurveML and is licensed/distributed by its authors. BIPED and BSDS500 are not redistributed by this repository. Review and follow each dataset's terms before use. BIPED is described by its authors as available for non-commercial research, teaching, publications, and personal experimentation.
